# Code of Conduct

MyCosmo is a friendly place to learn about space and about iOS development.

## Our standard

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or trolling.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block contributors who break these rules. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the maintainer through the contact options in the app's About screen.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
